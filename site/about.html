What is Open World Discovery?

We use Open World Discovery as a broad organizing term for research on acquiring, constructing, and revising knowledge through exploration and learning in incompletely understood or changing worlds.

It brings open-world learning, scientific and causal discovery, discovery intelligence, self-evolving agents, recursive self-improvement, and open-endedness into a shared conversation. These communities retain their own definitions and objectives.

@@FRAMEWORK@@

From unknowns to verified knowledge.

An agent forms hypotheses, chooses informative actions, gathers evidence, and revises its account of the world. The question, representation, or mechanism may itself need to change as an investigation develops.

We connect three objectives: building discovery agents, developing environments for meaningful investigation, and learning and evaluating discovery capabilities. Finding useful knowledge and improving the discovery procedure are related objectives, each requiring its own evidence.
